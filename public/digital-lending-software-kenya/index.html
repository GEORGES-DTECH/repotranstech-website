Automate & Scale Mobile Lending in Kenya

Digital credit provision in Kenya demands high speed, accuracy, and robust automated workflows. Modern borrowers expect rapid approval decisions, direct mobile money disbursements, and convenient repayment methods through M-Pesa.

Repotrans Digital Lending Software provides digital credit providers (DCPs), fintech startups, and online lenders with an end-to-end management backbone. From receiving loan requests to processing B2C payouts and managing automated SMS repayment prompts, our platform simplifies digital portfolio execution.

By removing manual intervention from routine disbursements and repayment tracking, Repotrans allows digital lenders to safely increase loan volumes while enforcing strict risk controls and loan limit rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Repotrans support M-Pesa B2C disbursements?

Yes, Repotrans supports M-Pesa B2C integration to send approved loan amounts straight to borrower mobile numbers.

How does automatic loan reconciliation work?

Incoming Paybill transactions are parsed instantly by our integration layer, matching payments against the borrower's loan account and updating balances in real time.

Can we set up automated SMS payment reminders?

Yes, automated SMS messages can be scheduled to notify borrowers prior to due dates, on the due date, and if an account becomes overdue.
