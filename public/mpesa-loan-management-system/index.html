Seamless M-Pesa Integration for Kenyan Lenders

M-Pesa is the primary channel for loan repayments and disbursements across Kenya. However, manually checking bank statements, updating ledger spreadsheets, and trying to match unknown transaction IDs consumes endless administrative hours and introduces costly human errors.

An integrated M-Pesa loan management system connects your lending software directly with Safaricom payment channels. Repotrans automatically matches incoming Paybill collections directly to borrower account ledgers, instantly updates loan balances, and sends automated payment confirmation messages.

Whether you operate a credit-only microfinance institution, a digital loan platform, or a SACCO, integrating M-Pesa into your core software ensures total financial accuracy and smoother collection operations.

M-Pesa Integration Features

Everything you need to automate mobile money workflows for your lending business.

Real-Time C2B Paybill Matching

Automatically match incoming customer Paybill repayments to the correct borrower account using national ID, mobile number, or account numbers.

Automated B2C Loan Disbursements

Disburse approved loans straight to borrower M-Pesa accounts instantly upon loan approval, complete with transaction logging.

One-Click STK Push Repayments

Initiate instant STK push prompts directly to borrower phones, allowing them to pay exact installment amounts by entering their M-Pesa PIN.

Unmatched Payment Management

Easily review payments made with wrong account numbers in a structured audit queue to assign them to correct borrowers without losing track.

Instant Repayment SMS Confirmations

Automatically send branded SMS balance receipts to borrowers as soon as their M-Pesa repayment is processed by the system.

Comprehensive Transaction Logs

Maintain complete records of all M-Pesa receipt IDs, dates, phone numbers, and matched account entries for seamless audit trail preparation.

Benefits of Automated M-Pesa Loan Reconciliation

What to Consider When Selecting an M-Pesa Loan System

When implementing M-Pesa integration, ensure the platform supports both C2B (repayments) and B2C (disbursements) APIs, handles mismatched account numbers efficiently, and maintains an unalterable transaction audit trail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Repotrans integrate with our existing Paybill?

Yes. Repotrans connects directly with your official Safaricom M-Pesa Paybill or Till numbers using standard Daraja APIs.

What happens if a client enters the wrong account number?

Payments with invalid account details are routed to an administrative suspense queue where staff can easily reassign the funds to the correct borrower profile.

Is STK Push supported for instant repayments?

Yes, Repotrans supports STK Push prompts so borrowers can pay back loans directly without typing in Paybill numbers manually.
